Frequently asked questions

How do I book a service?

Go to Find Services, choose a professional, select your date and time, add your address, then confirm the request.

How are workers verified?

In a live platform, the cooperative would verify identity and skill documents. This prototype only displays demo verification badges.

How do payments work?

This prototype simulates payment status only. No real money is collected or transferred.

What if I need to cancel?

Open My Bookings and choose Cancel on a booking that has not been completed.

Raise a support ticket

Describe the issue and the team can follow up in a connected production system.

Safety reminder

For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. This prototype is not an emergency-response service.
